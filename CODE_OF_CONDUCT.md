# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Kanga project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect towards other people.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation where appropriate.

Maintainers will consider the nature and severity of each incident and aim to respond fairly and proportionately. They are not required to disclose private reports or enforcement discussions publicly.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including the Kanga repository, its issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces officially representing the project. It also applies when an individual is officially representing Kanga in public spaces.

## Reporting

To report behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainer privately through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile:

https://github.com/vanmassey

Please include a description of the incident, where and when it occurred, and any relevant links or evidence. Do not post private or sensitive information in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

For security vulnerabilities, use the private vulnerability-reporting process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), rather than a public issue.
